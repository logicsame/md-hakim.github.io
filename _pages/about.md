I am an AI researcher working on **reasoning efficiency in large language models**: how the way a model is trained decides how well it spends compute at inference time. I am the **sole author of a NeurIPS 2026 main-track paper**, which shows across 50 models that training quality, more than parameter count or token budget, sets when extra reasoning stops paying off.
