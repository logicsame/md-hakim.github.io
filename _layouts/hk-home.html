Research

{{ content }}

  • Training quality and test-time reasoning

    When do extra reasoning tokens help, waste compute, or hurt accuracy? Across 50 models from 0.5B to 685B parameters, training methodology sets the boundary, more than size or token budget.

    NeurIPS 2026 paper

  • Reinforcement learning for reasoning

    Reward signals for RL post-training that teach reasoning models to stop once they have the answer, instead of over-generating until the budget runs out.

  • Looped language models

    Models that reuse the same layers several times. Should a model think deeper or longer, and can it choose its own number of loops for each input?
